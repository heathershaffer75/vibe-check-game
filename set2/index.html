Vibe Check – Red/Yellow/Green Flags (Set 2)

How to use: Read the profile together, discuss, then click the buttons to reveal Red/Yellow/Green flags and “Why it matters.” Finish with the reflection questions.

HighVibeHealer, 34
"Good energy only—feet on the ground, heart in the sky."
Serene, ritual-loving, and deeply spiritual, HighVibeHealer lives by cycles of meditation, moon-bathing, and crystal routines. They radiate calm and value inner alignment, though conflict can feel like a threat to that peace. They’re learning that accountability and repair can be sacred, too. Their vibe attracts attention—but avoiding hard emotions can leave a partner feeling dismissed or alone.
Favorite Movie: Arrival
Fictional Character: Iroh (Avatar: The Last Airbender)
Interests: Breathwork, sound baths, plant care, nature walks, tea ceremonies
Blind Spot: Spiritual bypassing when emotions feel heavy
Calls conflict “low vibe” and avoids it
Stays calm but dismisses messy emotions
Grounded, reflective, growth-oriented
Spiritual tools help calm emotions—but real repair takes talking things through.
Reflection Questions:
  • Would you want a partner who avoids conflict to protect “good vibes”? Why or why not?
  • Have you ever tried to “zen” your way around a fight instead of talking it through?
  • What does spiritually aligned accountability look like for you?
  • If HighVibeHealer were your roommate here, what would be helpful and what might be frustrating?
